# SGit-AI__Website__Newsroom__2026__Oct__PHP__site

The main UI: the homes, the briefings, the questions, the stories, the tools, the risk graph, the views, for the newsroom's site on the two critical PHP flaws of October 2026 (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, "php2shell"), served at **https://php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/**. It is staged at https://site.php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai/ until the cutover, when php.oct.2026.newsroom.sgit.ai moves here; until then it asks search engines not to index it.

One of six repositories: press (the build, the gate, the release), data (the API), refs, updates, team and site. Its pages are the sections every section the other sites do not own; every other address of the site answers here with a redirect to the site that serves it.
