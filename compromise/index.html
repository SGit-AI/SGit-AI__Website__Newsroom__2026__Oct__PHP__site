What would change the statement

  • A named organisation saying it was compromised through php2shell, in its own notice, filing or statement.
  • GMO, ČVUT or DMM stating a cause.
  • CISA naming a victim, in its catalog, its updated PHP alert or elsewhere. Its KEV listing of 26 October (both CVEs) holds evidence of exploitation and names none, so it did not change this statement.
  • A regulator's breach notice (for example under GDPR or Australia's Notifiable Data Breaches scheme) naming the flaw.
  • A security vendor naming a victim, with the victim's consent.

The source watch checks the official lists and the named organisations' pages every 30 minutes; this page is rebuilt from data/compromise.json when the evidence changes.

How this page is made

Every row is in data/compromise.json. Rows with sources cite captures in the evidence vault, which the build checks. Rows marked source watch cite a dated snapshot folder in the source-watch vault (7r83qic5); they move into the evidence vault as the evidence curator promotes them. Statements read in another language carry the reader’s English translation beside the verbatim original in the watch’s translations/ files. The charts are drawn from the same rows when the site is built.
