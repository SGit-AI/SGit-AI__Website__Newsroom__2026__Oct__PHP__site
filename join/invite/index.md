# Invite someone: a link and a QR code, signed on this device

For a key that may invite (editor). Give the person a handle (never their name), pick a role and how long the invitation lasts, and this page signs it with this device's key and shows a QR code, a link and a share button. Nothing is stored anywhere: when they join, the invitation is checked against your key, and their key is enrolled.

This page needs JavaScript: the invitation is signed in your browser.
