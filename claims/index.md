# Every claim, and who makes it

In plain English Every statement made about the story, who made it, and how far it can be trusted, with links to the evidence.

70 claims: 20 confirmed, 35 reported, 9 disputed, 2 contradicted, 4 unverified. A claim is a sentence somebody said, with the party that said it, the captured sources it comes from, and one of five words for how far it can be trusted. The words are defined here, once, and the build refuses a claim whose sources are not in the manifest.

- confirmed two or more primary sources agree, or the fact was counted from a captured file
- reported one party says so, and it has not been contradicted
- disputed the sources disagree, and both readings are shown
- contradicted a primary source says otherwise
- unverified even the reporting outlet could not check it
- p/n primary sources among the n cited; a blocked capture is counted and marked
